The campaign exists to push every member to give more — their own progress and tier badges are the point. The Red vs Blue team challenge is an optional overlay: the same gifts also tally into the team they're directed at, and each team has its own milestone (its own target + badge). Turn it off and the individual campaign is unchanged.

Group hierarchy — every member belongs to a subgroup

Greater Accra Region ← campaign owner ├─ Accra Metro District → Ama · Esi · Abena └─ Tema District → Kofi · Yaw · Kwame

Each member's subgroup (district) is a real position in the hierarchy — resolved from group_members + group_closure. Note the districts cut across the Red/Blue teams: your subgroup (where you sit in the org) and your team (an ad hoc roster) are independent.
