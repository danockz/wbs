This is the hierarchical (subtree) flavour of a group project. It is still individual‑first — every member competes for their own tier badge. The team challenge’s teams are the region’s immediate subgroups (the districts) and membership is derived from the hierarchy — there are no hand‑picked rosters. A gift is credited to the subgroup the member belongs to (team_kind = 'group'). Compare with the ad hoc version, where teams are arbitrary rosters cutting across the tree.

Group hierarchy — the subgroups ARE the teams

Greater Accra Region ← campaign owner ├─ Accra Metro District ← competing team → Ama · Abena · Esi └─ Tema District ← competing team → Yaw · Kofi · Kwame

Teams are resolved by resolveTeams() → attributedTeams(): each member rolls up to whichever immediate child of the owner sits on their group’s ancestor path (via group_closure). No roster tables involved.
